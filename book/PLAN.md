# Book Plan — "My Life" (working title)

Status: **Waiting on the author's raw story material.**

## Locked-in decisions

| Setting | Choice |
|---|---|
| Format | Novelized version of real events — scenes, dialogue, atmosphere |
| POV | Dramatic scenes built from the author's memories |
| Tone | Dramatic & gritty |
| Length | ~15 chapters, ~15,000 words (medium) |
| Material source | The author will write out their story and hand it over |

## Where we are

1. ✅ Format, tone, and length chosen
2. ⬜ Author brain-dumps their life story into `my-story.md` (or just pastes it in chat)
3. ⬜ I draft a chapter outline from the material
4. ⬜ I write the full manuscript, chapter by chapter
5. ⬜ Title, cover page, final read-through

## Notes for the writer (me, later)

- Conflict is the engine: lean hard into the struggles and turning points.
- Novelization means real events rendered as scenes — sensory detail, dialogue reconstructed from memory, tension on every page.
- Gaps in the material get dramatized, but flagged for the author to verify.
- Ask whether names should be real or changed.
