# Safe's Playce — Activity Bank

Built around what @safesplayce already does: **word games + group chat + coping skills**. Everything else is an expansion lane for later.

## 1. THE CORE — word game formats (your signature)

| Format | How it plays on LIVE |
|---|---|
| **Spelling Bee** 🐝 | Your flagship. Difficulty ladder: warm-ups → real ones → "Playce Killers." Weekly champs, monthly tournament crown. |
| **Word Ladder** | Change one letter at a time to get from WARM → COLD. Chat co-pilots; you drive. |
| **Categories** | Scattergories-style: "things that calm you down, letter S." Double points for heart + smarts. |
| **Finish the Phrase** | "The night I got arrested…" — no wait, that's Tuesday's story time. "A watched pot…" Chat completes, funniest wins. |
| **Word of the Night** | One meaningful word per stream (e.g., "resilience") — spell it, define it, everyone uses it in a sentence about their week. |
| **Story Chain** | Each chatters one word/phrase; you weave it into an affirming silly story about the community. |
| **21 Questions: Feelings Edition** | Chat guesses your word — all words are feelings or coping tools. Sneaky psychoeducation. |
| **Group Chat Games** | What you already do — keep the group-chat energy: everyone's face, everyone's turn, nobody lurks alone. |
| **Trivia: Myth vs. Fact** | Gentle mental-health myth-busting (no diagnosing, no numbers, no graphic content). |
| **Rhyme Time / Freestyle Friday** | Rhyme chains around a feel-good theme; you attempt (and fail) a rap, chat wins. |

**Bee Night ladder structure (write it on the wall):**
Warm-up (everyone gets these) → Hive words (chat splits into teams) → Playce Killers (words that broke previous streams — name them after community members who missed them; loving roasts only)

## 2. Community rituals (the glue — use every stream)

| Ritual | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Rose / Bud / Thorn** | Check-in: rose = good thing, bud = something coming, thorn = hard thing. You go first, always. |
| **Worry Drop-Box** | Chat drops one worry. Support, not solutions. "That sounds heavy. You're not alone in it." |
| **Gratitude Wall** | One gratitude per person, read aloud slowly. End of month = highlight reel. |
| **Wins of the Week** | Any size counts. "I showered." "I made the call." Applaud everything. |
| **Take What You Need** | Chat types a number, you read the affirmation tied to it. |
| **Water Break Check** | On-camera water + stretch every ~50 min. Self-care, visible. |

## 3. Coping-skill segments (rotate in, 10–20 min, never the whole stream)

- **Toolbox Time:** box breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, progressive muscle relaxation, brain-dump journaling — demo it, then DO it together
- **Ask-a-Peer Q&A:** "How do you get through ___?" Answer as a peer with lived experience — never an expert. Disclaimer first.
- **Sunday-scaries / work-stress / holiday-loneliness talks** — over a slow game round
- **Personal story time (2–5 min, weekly):** your top-performing content is your truth (3.6K views on a personal short vs 478 average). One honest story per stream — the comeback, the hard year, what brought you back. This is your superpower. Use it.

## 4. Special events (monthly, Saturdays)

- **Monthly Spelling Bee Tournament** — brackets, crown, championship clip
- **Charity streams** — goal ladder on screen (AFSP, NAMI, TWLOHA, Crisis Text Line)
- **Collab/multi-guest lives** — bring back the community faces (@Reesethenyturtle & co.)
- **Milestone parties** — 7.5K → 8K → 10K celebrations, chat picks everything
- **Community awards night** — silly superlatives ("Most Likely To Calm The Chat")

## 5. Expansion lanes (only when the core feels automatic)

- Cozy solo games as background campfire (Stardew, PowerWash, Unpacking, Dorfromantik, Coffee Talk)
- Interactive party games (Gartic Phone, Skribbl.io, Jackbox, Kahoot)
- Fall Guys / Among Us viewer lobbies once there's a regular crew
- Monthly comfort-media club (game/album/show the community picks)

## 6. Just-chatting starters (while words load)

- "What's your comfort show — the one you've rewatched five times?"
- "One small thing that made today 2% better?"
- "What's a word that always makes you smile? Tonight we're spelling it."
- "Finish this: 'This week, I'm proud of myself for ___'"
- "What's something you're nervous about this week? Let's normalize it together."

## Content mix rule of thumb

> **Support is the seasoning, not the meal.** ~70% game energy and laughter, ~30% heart and coping skills. They come for the bee. They stay because it's the one place on TikTok where somebody asks "what was your thorn today?" — and waits for the answer.
