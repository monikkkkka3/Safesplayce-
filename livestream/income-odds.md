## The ethics line (non-negotiable)

**Never pressure vulnerable viewers to spend.** No gift goals framed as "support the mission," no "we're $10 away" bars, no guilt. Let people give because they want to — and say that out loud on stream: "Your presence is the donation. Gifts are optional and never expected here." A support community that monetizes guilt dies; one that guards its people gets carried by them.
