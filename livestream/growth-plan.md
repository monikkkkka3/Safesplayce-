# Safe's Playce — Growth Plan (Comeback Edition)

Starting point (audited Aug 2026): **7.43K followers · 333 videos · 6.71% follower engagement ("terrific") · ~478 avg views/video · just back from a ~1 year break · LIVE + gaming incentive programs already unlocked.**

The honest diagnosis: you don't have a growth problem, you have a **rhythm problem**. The love is there (6.71%!). The schedule wasn't. Fix rhythm → the rest compounds.

## Phase 1 — Comeback Month (weeks 1–4)

**Goal: 12 streams completed, 12 clips posted, schedule in bio. Nothing else.**

- Hit Tue/Thu/Sat (+ optional Sun) without exception — short streams are fine, missed streams are not
- Schedule graphic pinned + announced as a short BEFORE week 1
- 1 clip short within 24 hrs of every stream, captioned with the next stream time
- Personally re-invite the old regulars (comments, tags, duets)
- **Weekly personal story short** — your truth outperforms everything else you post (3.6K vs 478 avg). Lean in.
- Success = consistency, not numbers. If peak viewers stay tiny all month, you still won.

## Phase 2 — Habit Season (months 2–3)

**Goal: convert 7.4K followers into a live regulars base; rebuild feed reach.**

- **The flywheel:** every stream → best-moment clip → clip ends with "Bee Night, Tuesday 7 PM" → clip viewers come to live → their moments become clips. Repeat forever.
- Double posting cadence: ~12 shorts/month → **20+/month** (schedule promos + clips + 1 personal story/week)
- Launch the **monthly Spelling Bee Tournament** as your flagship event; clip the finals
- Recruit 2–3 mods from regulars; hand them the safety doc
- Targets by end of month 3: **avg live peak 2–3x your current**, returning-viewer names you know by heart, follower count drifting toward 8K without chasing it

## Phase 3 — Compound Season (months 4–6)

**Goal: 10K followers + a self-sustaining community.**

- 10K unlocks bigger doors (Creator Rewards needs 10K + 100K views/30 days — the clip flywheel from Phase 2 is what gets you there)
- Monthly collab or guest stream (therapist Q&A with clear "general info, not therapy" framing; other mental-health creators)
- One charity stream per quarter — purpose events bind communities
- Start the expansion lanes (activities.md §5) ONLY if the core formats run without you sweating
- Consider a Discord "between streams" space once regulars ask for it (don't build it before they ask)

## Metrics that matter (in order)

1. **Streams completed vs. scheduled** — the only comeback-month metric
2. **Returning viewers** (names you recognize) — the community pulse
3. **Avg watch time** — the algorithm's favorite signal
4. **Clips posted per stream** — your reach engine
5. Peak viewers, new followers, diamonds — scoreboard, not strategy

## If the numbers stall (month 3+)

- Same viewers but no new ones → problem is the clip flywheel, not the live. Post more clips, better hooks (first 2 seconds = the funniest/most heartfelt moment).
- Views on clips but no live turnout → problem is conversion: end every clip with the day + time, say it on stream, put it in bio.
- You're exhausted → problem is the plan: cut Sunday, shorten to 90 min. A sustainable 3 > a heroic 4 that breaks you. Last break lasted a year; protect this one.
