**Handle:** @safesplayce · **Platform:** TikTok LIVE · **Cadence:** 3–4 streams/week, 2–3 hrs · **Timezone:** Central (Dallas)

## Non-negotiables (read monthly)

1. **Same days, same times, every week — in your bio.** Your followers already love you (6.71%!); they just need to know where to show up.
2. **The disclaimer, every stream:** "This is peer support, not professional help. If you're in crisis, the resources are pinned."
3. **The stream ends when the stream ends.** No counseling in DMs. Redirect to resources.
4. **You came back from a year off — protect the comeback.** A night off announced in advance keeps the promise; silence breaks it.
