## 6. Myth or Fact? 🧠 (read the statement, chat votes, reveal)

1. "Anxiety is just being dramatic." → **MYTH.** Anxiety is a real stress response — the body's alarm system, not a character flaw.
2. "You can just snap out of depression." → **MYTH.** It's not a mood switch; recovery takes support, time, and often professional help.
3. "Therapy is only for people in crisis." → **MYTH.** Plenty go for growth and maintenance — a gym for your mind.
4. "Mental health struggles are rare." → **MYTH.** Roughly 1 in 5 US adults experiences one in a given year. You're never the only one in the room.
5. "Asking for help is weakness." → **MYTH.** It's a skill — and the strong move.
6. "Self-care is selfish." → **MYTH.** You can't pour from an empty cup.
7. "Crying means you're weak." → **MYTH.** It's a healthy release — your body decompressing.
8. "Positive thinking cures depression." → **MYTH.** Positivity can ride along; it can't drive.
9. "Kids can't struggle with mental health." → **MYTH.** They can — and early support helps a lot.
10. "You need a diagnosis to benefit from coping tools." → **MYTH.** Breathing, movement, and sleep help everyone.
11. "Exercise is one of the most evidence-backed mood lifters." → **FACT.** Movement is legit medicine for mood.
12. "Sleep affects your mental health." → **FACT.** Sleep and mood run on the same wiring.
13. "Mental health has many causes — biology, life experiences, environment." → **FACT.** No single cause, no single blame.
14. "Talking about feelings makes them worse." → **MYTH.** Naming feelings reliably lowers their intensity.
15. "Connection is a real protective factor." → **FACT.** (That's the whole point of this playce.)
