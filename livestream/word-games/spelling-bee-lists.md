# Safe's Playce — Spelling Bee Word Lists 🐝

Five tiers, themed around the community: the lower tiers are words your chat lives by, the top tiers are the ones that broke people. Definitions included for Tier 3+ (chat will ask). Say the word → use it in a sentence → chat spells.

## How to run it (Bee Night, ~60–75 min)

1. **Warm-up (Tier 1, 10 min):** everyone who spells it "wins." No eliminations. Gets chat typing.
2. **Hive round (Tier 2, 15 min):** chat splits in two — first correct spelling wins the point.
3. **Real ones (Tier 3, 15 min):** correct = 2 pts. First to 10 carries the crown image.
4. **Sting round (Tier 4, 15 min):** 3 pts. Miss it and you're "buzzed" (loving roast, back next round).
5. **Playce Killers (Tier 5, 10 min):** 5 pts each, definitions first. Chaos. Clip it.
6. Crown the weekly champion → they pick next week's word *category*.

**House rules:** No elimination shaming — a miss gets "the hive forgives you" and a heart. Words missed live on as named "Playce Killers" (e.g., the @-named word that beat everybody).

## Bonus formats

- **A-to-Z Co-op:** chat must spell one coping/feel-good word for every letter of the alphabet, together. Team vs. the clock.
- **Backwards bee:** you spell it backwards, chat unscrambles the real word.
- **Category killer:** all words must fit tonight's theme (sleep words, nature words, comfort foods).
