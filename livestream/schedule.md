# Safe's Playce — Weekly Schedule

All times **Central**. Evenings anchor to TikTok's peak live window (6–9 PM local). Adjust ±1 hr to fit your life, then **never move them again** — and put the final times in your TikTok bio on day one.

## Weekly grid

| Day | Time (CT) | Show | Length | Content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tue** | 7:00–9:00 PM | **Bee Night** 🐝 | 2 hrs | Spelling bee rounds + rose/bud/thorn check-in |
| **Thu** | 7:00–9:00 PM | **Game Night** | 2 hrs | Rotating word games WITH chat (see activities.md) |
| **Sat** | 3:00–5:00 PM | **Saturday Playce** | 2–3 hrs | Flagship: bee tournament, monthly themes, milestones |
| **Sun** *(optional)* | 6:00–8:00 PM | **Slow Sunday** | 2 hrs | Wind-down: coping skill of the week, gratitude, gentle games |

**Why this shape:** Tue/Thu/Sat = the committed 3. Sunday is the flex day — bonus streams, collabs, or rest. Two rest days between streams protects the comeback (last break lasted a year; burnout prevention IS the strategy).

## COMEBACK MONTH — your first 4 weeks back

You're re-entering after a year off. The only goal for month one: **make the schedule undeniable.**

- **Week 0 (before restarting):** Post the schedule graphic as a short: "The Playce is open again. Tuesdays. Thursdays. Saturdays. 7 PM." Pin it.
- **Weeks 1–4:** Hit every single slot, even if it's just you and 5 people. The algorithm and the regulars are both re-learning your rhythm.
- **Every stream this month:** mention the schedule out loud twice — mid-stream and at the end.
- **Post 1 short within 24 hrs of every stream** (best moment clip). This is non-negotiable during comeback month; it's how lapsed followers learn you're back.
- **Re-engage the regulars by name.** You already have community (ReesetheNYturtle, Pass Ma 3 The Hookah, WINDMANKNOWS, mommy taco-era friends). Name-drop, tag, invite back personally.
- **Do NOT chase follower count this month.** Chase: streams completed, clips posted, returning viewers.

## Run-of-show templates

Every stream opens and closes the same way. Rituals are what make a stream feel like a *place* — and they take pressure off you: half the show runs itself.

### Standard 2-hour template

| Clock | Segment | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | **Welcome + check-in question** | Greet by name. Rose/bud/thorn. One-breath rules recap. |
| 0:10–0:50 | **Block 1 — the game** | The night's main format (see below) |
| 0:50–1:00 | **Grounding break** | Water, stretch, one breathing exercise — on camera. |
| 1:00–1:40 | **Block 2 — the game, escalated** | Finals / harder rounds / challenge stakes |
| 1:40–1:55 | **Community corner** | Wins of the week, gratitude wall, shoutouts |
| 1:55–2:00 | **Wind-down** | Closing affirmation, schedule reminder, "go drink water. See you Thursday." |

### Tuesday — Bee Night 🐝
- Your signature. Spelling bee rounds by difficulty ladder; chat competes (typed answers or unprompted spelling).
- **Ladder structure:** Warm-up words → Real ones → "Playce Killers" (the words that broke previous streams — name them after community members)
- Track a **weekly champion** + crown a monthly "Champion Bee" on Saturdays. Champions pick a word category next stream.
- Mid-stream: **Worry Drop-Box** (chat drops a worry, support not solutions)
- End: one coping tool of the week, demonstrated for 2 minutes

### Thursday — Game Night
- Rotate formats so Thursdays never repeat within a month — the full menu lives in activities.md (word ladders, categories/Scattergories-style, trivia, "finish the phrase," story chain, group chat games)
- Monthly leaderboard across all Thursday games; silly prizes (you draw their icon, they pick next week's game, they name a word)
- **Rule of the night:** laughter is the medicine. This is the relief-valve night.
- 15-min "grown-ups table": one real-talk topic over a slow round

### Saturday — Saturday Playce (flagship)
- **Week 1:** Monthly Spelling Bee TOURNAMENT — brackets, Champion Bee crown, highlights clipped
- **Week 2:** Community choice — chat votes the format all week (polls in bio/comments)
- **Week 3:** Special event — collab, guest conversation (therapist/advocate Q&A with clear framing), or charity stream
- **Week 4:** Month wrap-up — wins montage, gratitude wall reading, monthly awards (silly superlatives)
- End every Saturday teasing next week + the month's theme

### Sunday — Slow Sunday (optional)
- Lo-fi, low light, low stakes. "Beating the Sunday scaries together."
- Coping skill of the week (deeper version), gentle word games, planning talk
- Close with a 10-min guided wind-down: breathing, gratitude, "one thing you're looking forward to"

## Monthly themes (rotate on Saturdays)

1. **New Season, New Playce** — relaunch energy, community names things (a word category, a segment, the bee mascot)
2. **Toolbox Month** — a different coping skill demoed every Saturday, word categories themed around calm
3. **Community Month** — viewers pick everything; vote on games, words, titles
4. **Giving Back** — charity stream (mental-health orgs), goal ladder on screen
5. **Comfort Month** — "words that feel like a hug"; community stories month
