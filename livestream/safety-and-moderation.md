# Safe's Playce — Safety & Moderation Kit

The most important document in this folder. A support-flavored community without guardrails will hurt someone — including you. Read before every stream week until it's memorized.

## 1. What this stream IS and IS NOT (say it, pin it, repeat it)

> **IS:** A peer-support gaming space. Real people, real lived experience, zero judgment.
> **IS NOT:** Therapy, crisis intervention, or professional advice. No one here is your therapist — including the host.

**Opening disclaimer (say every stream, warmly):**
"Quick housekeeping, fam: this is a peer support space, not professional help. I'm a person who's been through it, not a therapist. If you're in crisis tonight, the resources are pinned in the chat — please use them, and stay with us."

## 2. Pinned crisis resources (US — your audience is local-first)

Pin in chat, put in bio, keep on a graphic overlay:

- **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** — call or text **988** (24/7, US)
- **Crisis Text Line** — text **HOME** to **741741**
- **SAMHSA National Helpline** — **1-800-662-4357** (mental health/substance use treatment referral)
- **The Trevor Project** — **1-866-488-7386** (LGBTQ+ youth)
- **Veterans Crisis Line** — text **838255**
- **International viewers:** findahelpline.com

## 3. Chat rules (pin nightly, mods enforce)

1. Be kind. We're all carrying something.
2. **No graphic detail** of self-harm, methods, or numbers (weights, doses, etc.). Feelings yes, specifics no.
3. No diagnosing, prescribing, or "you should just ___" advice.
4. No pressure on anyone to share more than they want.
5. No politics/religion pile-ons; no doom-scrolling news talk.
6. What's shared here stays here — but assume nothing online is private.
7. Mods may remove posts and redirect you to pinned resources out of care, not judgment.

## 4. Moderation plan

**Crew:** Recruit 2–3 mods (regulars you trust) covering your stream hours. Even one is enough to start. Give them this document.

**Auto-filter keywords to block** (TikTok creator tools → keyword filters): graphic self-harm terms, method terms, slurs, spam, competing stream promos. Keep the list private — it's a seatbelt, not a talking point.

**Mod script for a crisis message in chat:**
1. Remove the graphic message, don't screenshot/repeat it.
2. Mod posts pinned resources in a reply: "We see you and you matter. Please reach out — 988 (call/text) or text HOME to 741741. That's the strongest move, and we'll be here after."
3. Host (if streaming): address it *briefly and calmly*, without quoting details: "Hey — someone in chat is having a really hard time. Thank you for being here. Please use the pinned numbers; they're the best help that exists. Everyone else, send a heart."
4. Never promise one-on-one help. Never take it to DMs. Redirect, every time.

**Bans:** immediate for trolls baiting self-harm talk, hate, or spamming "advice" after warnings.

## 5. Platform reality check

TikTok moderates mental-health content aggressively. Graphic descriptions of self-harm can get the **stream cut and the account restricted** — one more reason rule #2 exists. Keep the framing *recovery and support*, always. Avoid TikTok's restricted terms in your titles/hashtags; say "self-care," "support," "coping," not shock words.

## 6. Protecting YOU (this section is not optional)

Compassion fatigue is an occupational hazard of this niche. Burned-out you = no stream for the people who need it.

- **Hard stop at stream end.** Say goodnight, end, close the app. The after-stream DM pileup will wait.
- **No counseling in DMs. Ever.** Copy-paste reply: "I care, and I'm not the right help — 988 or 741741 is. See you Tuesday." A DM you can't close becomes a weight you carry alone.
- **Post-stream ritual:** 15 minutes of nothing — walk, snack, shower — before you touch comments or analytics.
- **Your own support:** keep your own therapist/support system. You can't be the pillar if the pillar has no foundation.
- **Monthly audit (one honest question):** "Do I still look forward to stream days?" If two months in a row the answer is no — restructure the show, not your life around it.
- **A bad mental-health week for you = pre-record or skip.** Announce, rest, return. The community respects a leader who models boundaries.
